# A web-based inventory system for a theoretical small auto parts company with multiple locations.
## For CSCE4350 Database Systems course project

Jessica Clark
Hazel Rexrode

# Information stored:

```text
Store Locations
- Address
- Name

Item Definition
- Name
- ID
- Price

Inventory of Items
- What Item
- What Location
- Quantity

Employees
- What Employee
- Name
- Job Title
- Company Permissions

Purchase Orders (B2B)
- PO#
- What items are being ordered
- when they were ordered
- how many of them have arrived
- what store they are being sent to

Customer Orders
- What items are being ordered
- what store they are being ordered from
- fulfilment status

In Store Sales History
- Ticket #
- Sale Date
- Items on the sale
- price items sold for

Employee Timecards
- employee #
- clock in time
- clock out time
```

# Major Features
```text
Employee Timecards
Purchase Orders & Item Receivings
In-store Point-of-Sale
    - including paymnet
Customer Database
Online Ordering
    - including payment
Customer item browsing/searching
Business Reports
    - PO status
    - Customer Order Management
    - Sales Report
```
